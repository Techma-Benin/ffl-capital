Subject: Approved lead routing lifecycle

Approved routing lifecycle for Integrity / platform partner alignment:

0–24 hours
- Try Integrity Realtime (ILC) only.
- Retry definitive rejections while inside the acceptance window.
- Do not automatically match platform partners during this window.

24–48 hours
- Attempt either platform partner or ILC Storefront first, based on admin-configured priority.
- Only try the other route after the primary route definitively fails.
- A pending asynchronous Integrity posting blocks fallback.

48 hours–30 days
- Platform partners only.
- Do not automatically retry Realtime or Storefront.

30+ days
- Preserve existing aged marketplace eligibility, including leads already sold live.

One automatic live sale
- A successful Realtime, Storefront, or partner sale prevents all further automatic live routing.
- Only an explicit admin action may redeliver a live-sold lead.

Deployment note (Phase 2 implementation)
- Lifecycle routing ships behind an admin feature flag that defaults to OFF.
- Azure ping credentials are env-only and must be rotated before live enablement.
